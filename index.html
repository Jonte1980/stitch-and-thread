Simple process

Three steps to your
perfect pattern

No software to install. No account to create. Open your browser, upload your photo, and your pattern is ready.

01

Upload your image

Any photo works — portraits, pets, landscapes, logos. JPG, PNG or WebP, any size.

02

Adjust & generate

Choose your stitch count, color palette, thread brand and technique. Click generate.

03

Export & stitch

Download a multi-page PDF with chart, legend, and shopping list. Start stitching today.

04

Stitch with joy

Use the built-in progress tracker to mark each stitch. Watch your creation come to life.

What's included

Everything you need
to bring your ideas to life

Built by stitchers, for stitchers. Every feature exists because someone needed it in their craft room.

FREE

Pattern generator

Cross-stitch (DMC, up to 80pt, 15 colors) and Miyuki Loom (up to 20×20 beads, free shape). Powered by Lab-space color matching.

FREE

Portrait & pet mode

Specialized preprocessing with edge sharpening, skin/fur smoothing and background simplification for centered subjects.

FREE

Realistic view & Aida selector

See your pattern as it will really look when stitched. Choose fabric count and see the physical size in cm.

FREE

Illustration / anime mode

Optimised processing for flat-colour artwork, illustrations and anime-style images. Clean edges, vivid palette.

FREE

PNG export & offline PWA

Export your pattern as PNG (with watermark on Free). Install as an app on any device — works fully offline, no data ever stored.

PRO

All 4 thread brands

Anchor (444 colors), Cosmo (500), Madeira (381) — plus DMC. Switch brands at any time without regenerating.

PRO

PDF export

Multi-page chart: bold 10-stitch grid, symbol legend, skein/tube count per color, and a printable shopping list.

PRO

Brush editor & Thread Box

Paint cells directly on the canvas. Change any color across the whole pattern. Save your personal thread inventory.

PRO

Shopping list export

Export your exact buy list as .txt. Amazon affiliate links for every DMC color — buy everything in one click.

PRO

Progress mode

Tap stitches to mark them done. Live counter shows stitches completed, remaining, and overall percentage.

PRO

Save projects to Drive

Save your pattern, color overrides and stock directly to Google Drive. Open from any device, anytime.

PRO — Miyuki

Brick Stitch & Peyote

All offset techniques with correct canvas rendering and PDF export. All shapes: Earring, Bracelet, Necklace. No size limits.
